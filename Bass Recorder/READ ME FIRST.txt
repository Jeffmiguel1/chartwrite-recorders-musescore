Bass Recorder · Chartwrite Sounds - Bass recorder for MuseScore 4 (free)
A real bass recorder, leveled note by note so no part of the range sticks out.

FILE
  .sf3  (1.2 MB) - for MuseScore 4

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Open MuseScore 4. Drag the .sf3 file from this folder onto the MuseScore window and click Install.
   (Or: quit MuseScore, copy it into Documents/MuseScore4/SoundFonts, then open MuseScore again.)
2. Open your score. Open the Mixer (View > Mixer, or F10).
3. On the bass recorder track, click the sound name, choose CW Recorder (Bass) and pick a preset from the list:
     Bass Recorder         - the main sound
     Bass Rec Vibrato      - with a gentle vibrato
     Bass Rec Staccato     - short, tongued notes
4. Press play.

HOW TO WRITE FOR IT
  Write normal bass recorder notation (it sounds an octave above written; MuseScore handles this). Range: F3 to F5 sounding (written F2 to F4).
  Recorders are quiet instruments: if it sits low next to other parts, raise its fader in the Mixer.

# Free Recorder Sounds for MuseScore 4 · Chartwrite Sounds

Soprano, alto, tenor and bass recorder SoundFonts (.sf3) for MuseScore 4, made from real recorder samples and leveled note by note. Free for any music you make, including commercial.

**Hear it:** [Greensleeves for recorder quartet (YouTube)](https://youtu.be/tf1nLB2nQWo) · MP3 + score in `Demos/`

## What's inside

| Instrument | Sounding range | Presets |
|---|---|---|
| Soprano Recorder | C5–C#7 | Soprano Recorder, Sop Rec Staccato |
| Alto Recorder | E4–G6 | Alto Recorder, Alto Rec Vibrato, Alto Rec Staccato |
| Tenor Recorder | C4–Eb6 | Tenor Recorder, Tenor Rec Vibrato, Tenor Rec Staccato |
| Bass Recorder | F3–F5 | Bass Recorder, Bass Rec Vibrato, Bass Rec Staccato |

Plus a ready-made recorder quartet template (`Template/`) and a Greensleeves demo (`Demos/`).

## Install (2 minutes)

1. **Download:** click the green **Code** button > **Download ZIP** (or get `Recorders.zip` under **Releases**).
2. Open MuseScore 4 and drag each `.sf3` file onto the MuseScore window, then click **Install**.
   (Or quit MuseScore, copy the four `.sf3` files into `Documents/MuseScore4/SoundFonts`, and reopen it.)
3. In your score, open the Mixer (F10), click the sound name on a recorder track > **SoundFonts** > **CW Recorder (Soprano / Alto / Tenor / Bass)**, and press play.

Fastest start: copy `Template/Recorders - Chartwrite Sounds Template.mscz` into `Documents/MuseScore4/Templates`, then File > New > choose it.

Recorders are quiet instruments: if they sit low next to other parts, raise their faders in the Mixer.

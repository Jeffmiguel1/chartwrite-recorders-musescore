Alto Recorder · Chartwrite Sounds - Alto recorder for MuseScore 4 (free)
A real alto recorder, leveled note by note so no part of the range sticks out.

FILE
  .sf3  (2.3 MB) - for MuseScore 4

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Open MuseScore 4. Drag the .sf3 file from this folder onto the MuseScore window and click Install.
   (Or: quit MuseScore, copy it into Documents/MuseScore4/SoundFonts, then open MuseScore again.)
2. Open your score. Open the Mixer (View > Mixer, or F10).
3. On the alto recorder track, click the sound name, choose CW Recorder (Alto) and pick a preset from the list:
     Alto Recorder         - the main sound
     Alto Rec Vibrato      - with a gentle vibrato
     Alto Rec Staccato     - short, tongued notes
4. Press play.

HOW TO WRITE FOR IT
  Write normal alto recorder notation. Range: E4 to G6 sounding.
  Recorders are quiet instruments: if it sits low next to other parts, raise its fader in the Mixer.
